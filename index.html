Privacy Policy — SSK App

Last Updated: July 2026

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Seminary of Sacred Knowledge, we are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your information with care and in accordance with Islamic principles of trust (Amanah) and ethical conduct. This policy describes how the SSK mobile app ("the App") handles information for our community.

1. Information We Collect

The App is designed so that you can use it without creating an account or providing any personal information. We may collect the following, only where noted:

  • Notification Token. To deliver prayer time reminders, announcements, and Q&A updates, your device is assigned a random notification token. This token is not linked to your name or identity unless you choose to provide it separately.
  • Q&A Submissions. If you submit a question through the App, you may optionally include your name. If left blank, your question appears publicly as "Anonymous." Seminary staff can always see who submitted a question, in keeping with our responsibility to respond thoughtfully and appropriately.
  • Contact Us & Feedback. If you reach out through the App's Contact Us or Feedback forms, any information you choose to share (such as your name, email, or message) is sent directly to Seminary staff to address your inquiry.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver prayer time, announcement, and Q&A notifications you've requested.
  • To allow Seminary staff to review and respond to community questions.
  • To respond to inquiries submitted through Contact Us or Feedback.
  • To maintain and improve the App's functionality.

3. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not require an account or login to use the App.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not use your information for advertising.
  • We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage

Information collected through the App is stored securely using Supabase, a third-party backend service provider, including any images uploaded for events or announcements.

5. Children's Privacy

Since the App does not require an account or personal information for its core features, it is designed to be safe for use by community members of all ages.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the App evolves. Changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date above.
